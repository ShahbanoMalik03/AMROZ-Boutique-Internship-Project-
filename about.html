ABOUT AMROZ

Our Story, Our Passion

Amroz Atelier transforms the poetry of nature into modern luxury couture. Rooted in South Asian craftsmanship, our signature is expressed through soft pastel hues, organic forms, intricate embroidery, and artful embellishment. Crisp silhouettes and impeccable finishing create a balance of delicacy and strength crafted for the modern woman who embodies confidence, sophistication, and effortless elegance.

OUR HISTORY

Amroz began with a simple dream:

To transform a passion for fashion into a celebration of individuality and timeless elegance. Founded by a young fashion designer and PIFD graduate, Amroz grew from her university journey, where years of exploring textiles, silhouettes, embroidery, and creative design shaped her vision. After graduating, she decided to turn that vision into her own brand—creating pieces that combine traditional craftsmanship with a modern couture aesthetic.
Her first collection, Lueur Despair, reflects this journey. Inspired by the contrast between light and shadow, the collection features sophisticated silhouettes, intricate detailing, and luxurious fabrics designed for unforgettable occasions.
Today, Amroz represents a growing vision of contemporary Pakistani fashion where every design is thoughtfully created, beautifully crafted, and made to tell its own story.
Amroz, Where creativity meets couture.

OUR MISSION

To make every woman feel extraordinary through thoughtfully designed couture — one unforgettable piece at a time.

At Amroz, we believe a dress should be more than something you wear. It should express who you are, celebrate your special moments, and leave a lasting impression.
Every piece we create is thoughtfully crafted with intricate details, refined silhouettes, and a passion for timeless elegance. Through Lueur Despair and our future collections, we aim to bring artistry, individuality, and confidence to every occasion.
